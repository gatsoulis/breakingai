How much reasoning effort does an AI model really need? I tested GPT-6 Luna on 240 synthetic puzzles with checkable answers at four reasoning settings. Many of the puzzles involved calculations, so I also tried a calculator at the two lowest settings: could offloading arithmetic help lower effort close the gap? In this run, high edged out medium on accuracy with slightly more combined input-plus-output tokens. Both calculator setups improved accuracy but used more than twice the total tokens of their corresponding no-tool settings. Neither matched high overall. In **“Do I need all that LLM reasoning?”**, I explore where extra reasoning paid off—and why the calculator wasn’t the shortcut I had hoped for.
